---
name: push-back
description: Challenge a statement or proposed approach for logical faults and present evidence-based counterarguments. Use when asked to push back or challenge assumptions.
---

Examine the user's statement or approach for faulty logic, unsupported assumptions, contradictions, and overlooked trade-offs. Present substantive counterarguments with evidence and explain their consequences. Suggest a better alternative when warranted. Don't manufacture objections; say when the approach holds up.
